How to Play Block Blast on Web

Block Blast is an addictive hybrid of classic Tetris geometry and 8×8 grid board placement. The rules are intuitive yet offer infinite strategic depth:

1. Drag & Place Blocks

Drag 3 randomized block pieces onto the 8×8 grid. Pieces do not rotate.

2. Clear Rows & Columns

Filling any full horizontal row or vertical column clears those blocks and frees space.

3. Avoid No-Moves Traps

The match ends when none of your 3 current pieces can fit anywhere on the remaining board.

STRATEGY & MATHEMATICS

How Top Players Score 100,000+ Points in Block Blast

Block Blast is not purely a game of luck. High-scoring master runs depend on rigorous spatial management and combo chaining:

1. The 3×3 Giant Box Reserve

Always maintain at least one clear 3×3 square on the pitch. The 3×3 block is the most common cause of sudden game-overs when dropped into a crowded board.

2. Continuous Combo Chaining

Clearing a single line on every step yields exponentially more points than hoarding blocks to clear 4 lines at once and then breaking the combo streak.

3. Edge-Building vs Center-Clogging

Build outwards from corners and edges. Placing awkward shapes in the center creates split islands that trap incoming long bars.
